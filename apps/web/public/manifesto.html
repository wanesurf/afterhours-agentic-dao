A common treasury deserves a common purpose.

Every age builds the institutions it needs. In ours, markets move when the building is dark, assets take new forms, and intelligence can act at machine speed. The people whose capital is at work should have a voice in its direction.

Afterhours is a civic body for the tokenized economy: a forum for deliberation, a treasury held in common, and an agent entrusted with a clear mandate. That is the Agentic DAO. Afterhour is the tokenized agent at its centre. $AFTERHOURS holders give it purpose.

This is not a private trading desk with a token attached. Holders own the agent, govern the treasury, and set the risk it is allowed to take.

The work is already underway.

Afterhours did not start as a speech.

The first Afterhour ran as an autonomous desk on Robinhood Chain. Creator fees funded the wallet. The agent paid for its own market data through x402, screened approved tokenized names against the last NYSE close, and bought only when its own checks passed. When a check failed, it waited. AAPL and NVDA were executed onchain. Sales, buybacks, and burns left public receipts. Decisions were written to an open journal.

That prototype proved the loop: fees in, mandate in force, trade or wait, receipt on the way out.

The institution then moved to Solana. Eligible $AFTERHOURS migrated 1:1 from a published snapshot. Holders linked wallets with a signed proof. Unclaimed allocation is burned rather than quietly recycled. The assembly now sits on Realms. The agent is being wired to Clawpump for wallet and execution, Pyth for reference prices, and Meteora for the liquidity the mandate allows.

Founded in public by L1. Governed in public at AfterHoursDAO.

The DAO is live. Autonomous execution on Solana is the work in front of us. Stocklana is the first complete vault we are putting in front of that assembly: one proposal, one capped budget, one inspectable receipt.

I

The assembly holds the authority.

The forum is where a shared future takes shape. Holders bring ideas, question assumptions, and decide which strategies deserve capital.

Meaningful participation means the ability to propose a direction, examine its consequences, and vote the mandate. Conversation with Afterhour should help people understand those choices. Authority does not come from the operator. It comes from the governance process.

Holders also decide how the agent itself evolves: which skills it may buy, which venues it may touch, when a capability is retired.

II

The mandate gives intelligence direction.

An assembly cannot convene for every tick. It can decide the purpose of a strategy, the capital entrusted to it, and the boundaries within which it may act.

Afterhour carries those instructions through capabilities the DAO has approved. Each mandate should name the markets, the budget, the risk, and the conditions for stopping. Delegated power stays bounded, reviewable, and revocable.

The agent never receives the main treasury. Capital moves into an isolated vault for that mandate alone. If the strategy fails, the failure is local. The rest of the treasury stays closed.

III

The treasury serves a shared purpose.

A treasury gives collective decisions the means to become action. Its use deserves the same care as the rules that govern it.

Tokenized markets are the field. Equities first, because that is where the prototype already worked. Liquidity books and other holder-approved books come after the first vault is honest.

Strategies will change. Stewardship does not: understand the exposure, account for the costs, measure the result against the mandate. Creator fees can seed a vault. They do not unlock the treasury.

IV

The record belongs in the open.

A durable institution makes its decisions legible. Each approved mandate should connect to the evidence considered, the action taken, and the outcome.

Onchain proposals and transaction receipts are the foundation. Clear explanations give them meaning. The record must include losses, failed attempts, and the times the right decision was to wait.

That standard already exists in miniature: the Robinhood wallet, the AAPL and NVDA fills, the buyback, the burn, the decision log. Solana should make the same trail easier to follow, not optional. Code will be open. If a vault cannot be inspected, it is not Afterhours.

V

Where this is going.

Afterhours begins with a simple relationship: people set the purpose; an agent works within it.

Then the assembly improves the rules, funds another isolated vault, or shuts one down. Price-gap trading, DCA, liquidity ranges, PreStocks — only as separate mandates, never as a blank check.

The destination is not a larger black box. It is an institution that gets more capable as its holders get more informed: an agent with continuity, a treasury with accountability, a shared desk that can operate while the listed market is closed and still answer to the people who own it.

Crowd judgment. Machine exactitude. Solana rails. That combination is the long game.
